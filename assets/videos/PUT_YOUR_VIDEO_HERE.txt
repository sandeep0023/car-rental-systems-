Put your background video file here and name it:  banner-bg.mp4

Where to get a free car/driving video (no login needed):
1. https://www.pexels.com/search/videos/car%20driving/
2. https://pixabay.com/videos/search/car%20driving/

Steps:
1. Download an MP4 video (choose a short one, 10-20 seconds loops best,
   under ~10MB is ideal so it loads fast).
2. Rename it to: banner-bg.mp4
3. Place it in this folder: carrental/assets/videos/
4. Refresh localhost/carrental — the homepage banner will now autoplay
   the video as its background instead of the static image.

If no video is placed here, the banner falls back to the original
banner-image.jpg automatically (via the poster attribute), so the site
never breaks.
